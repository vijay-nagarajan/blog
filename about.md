I am a professor in the Kahlert School of Computing at the University of Utah. I work on memory consistency models and cache coherence: how to specify them, how to synthesize hardware that implements them, and how to verify that it does. I am a co-author of *A Primer on Memory Consistency and Cache Coherence*.

This blog is for the things that do not fit a paper: results worth a short explanation, questions I find interesting, and the occasional opinion.
