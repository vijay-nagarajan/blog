This is a blog about computer architecture, programming languages and systems, with a focus on memory consistency and the protocols that enforce it, coherent or non-coherent.

I am a professor in the Kahlert School of Computing at the University of Utah ([website](https://users.cs.utah.edu/~vijay/)). My group's approach is [constructive](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~EWD/transcriptions/EWD02xx/EWD209.html) and [specification-directed](https://cacm.acm.org/opinion/who-builds-a-house-without-drawing-blueprints/): we derive implementations from precise specifications, so they are correct by construction. They are also efficient, because we implement only what the specification requires. I am a co-author of *A Primer on Memory Consistency and Cache Coherence*.

The blog is for things that may not fit a paper: smaller results, results that span multiple papers, and the occasional opinion.
