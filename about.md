This is a blog about computer architecture, programming languages, and computer systems, with a focus on memory consistency and the protocols that enforce it, coherent or non-coherent.

I am Vijay Nagarajan, a professor in the Kahlert School of Computing at the University of Utah ([website](https://users.cs.utah.edu/~vijay/)) and a co-author of *A Primer on Memory Consistency and Cache Coherence*. My group's approach is [constructive](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~EWD/transcriptions/EWD02xx/EWD209.html) and [specification-directed](https://cacm.acm.org/opinion/who-builds-a-house-without-drawing-blueprints/): we derive implementations from precise specifications, so they are correct by construction. They are also efficient, because we implement only what the specification requires.

The blog is for things that may not fit a paper: smaller results, results that span multiple papers, and the occasional opinion.
